# Curated inputs

These files are small public inputs used to compare address evidence with
chain-derived balances.

- `curated/blacklisted-addresses-2024-final.txt`: byte-for-byte copy of the
  final Harmony Ansible mint-response blacklist. It contains 71 lines and 70
  unique addresses.
- `curated/reported-wallet-theft-perpetrators.txt`: 17 Harmony addresses
  explicitly labeled as perpetrator-controlled in the supplied reports.
- `curated/reported-wallet-theft-perpetrators.csv`: role and report citation
  for those 17 addresses.

The original wallet-theft reports are not republished here. Their hashes and
the extraction rule are recorded in result provenance.

The complete 2021–2022 operational blacklist remains unavailable.
